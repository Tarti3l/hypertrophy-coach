# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

Delegated: Expo + React Native + TypeScript for a single native iOS/Android codebase, Supabase for the managed backend, and PostgreSQL as the source of truth. This was inferred from the request for a mobile app and an initial implementation; it remains easy to revise before release.

## Users

People beginning strength training from zero. They need a calm first-run experience that turns basic body and goal information into an understandable starting point for hypertrophy.

## Product Purpose

Teach sustainable muscle-gain fundamentals and help beginners build consistent training, nutrition, hydration, recovery, and sleep habits. Success means a user completes onboarding, understands their initial targets, and returns to follow a simple plan.

## Positioning

The product makes hypertrophy education actionable in small, connected decisions: an explainable estimate becomes a training, nutrition, recovery, and progress routine rather than a generic workout list.

## Operating Context

The app is used on a phone before or around gym sessions and at home. The current first-run flow gathers age, height, current weight, prior knowledge, training frequency, and short-/long-term goals, then creates starting calorie and macro targets.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Freemium mobile product; paid-entitlement enforcement is a later module.
- First release supports iOS and Android with an offline-first onboarding draft.
- The macro result is an educational estimate, not a diagnosis, treatment plan, or medical advice.
- Exact date of birth is intentionally not collected in onboarding; only age is needed for this estimate.
- Assumption: onboarding is available to users aged 14+; legal age gating and regional privacy policy are open product decisions.

## Brand Commitments

The requested experience is extremely clean, minimal, and free from distraction. Language is practical, reassuring, and avoids aggressive body or transformation claims.

## Evidence on Hand

No identity assets, content library, user research, pricing, medical review, or exercise-media library has been supplied. Future screens must not fabricate proof, results, or clinical endorsement.

## Product Principles

- Explain the recommendation enough that a beginner can act on it.
- Prefer a small next step over overwhelming configuration.
- Treat consistency, recovery, and safety as part of progress.
- Keep personal data minimal and owned by the user.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Dynamic Type / Android font scaling, screen readers, sufficient contrast, and touch targets appropriate to each platform. The formula offers an undisclosed option rather than forcing sex data.
